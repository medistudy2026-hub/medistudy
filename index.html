💡 Why I built MediStudy▾

Hi! I'm Ankit Lal Sahu, a 3rd year MBBS student at Ulyanovsk State University in Russia. I built MediStudy to make studying easier for myself and my classmates — AI explanations, organized notes, and lecture videos all in one clean app.

If it helps even one student understand a topic better before an exam — it was worth building. 🩺

🔒 Your Data & Privacy▾

Here's exactly what MediStudy collects, and why:

When you sign in: your name and email, via Google or Email sign-up.

In your profile: your name, course, and optionally your gender & age — used to personalize your experience and show you the right course's content.

Study activity: your quiz scores, study hours (tracked automatically while the app is open), saved notes, and Batch Chat messages — used for the Leaderboard and to keep your progress synced across your devices.

Who can see it: Only I (the developer) can see registered students' email. Other students only ever see your name on the Leaderboard, in Batch Chat, and in Feedback — never your email or profile details.

This app has no ads, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone outside MediStudy.
